Not every customer has three minutes to spare at the counter. Some are in a rush, some just want to get home, some would rather answer on their own phone later that evening. If your feedback strategy only lives on a tablet by the door, you’re missing all of them.

That’s why every FeedbackPro device license now comes with **two active surveys** instead of one — built to cover both the moment of the visit and everything after it.

## One License, Two Ways to Listen

-   **On-device survey:** runs right on your kiosk or tablet, capturing reactions while the experience is still fresh.

-   **Shareable survey:** the same energy, distributed by email or QR code, so customers can respond whenever it’s convenient for them.

No extra licenses, no separate tools to manage — just more ways in.

## QR Codes: Feedback That Meets Customers Where They Are

Print it on a receipt, a table tent, a poster by the door, the packaging itself — customers scan and go. No app to download, no site to hunt for. It’s the lowest-friction way to turn “I have a second” into an actual response.

## Email Surveys: For When the Good Stuff Comes Later

Some of your best feedback shows up after someone’s had time to think. Post-appointment check-ins, post-purchase follow-ups, post-stay reviews — email surveys let you ask once the dust has settled, and customers tend to write more when they’re not rushing.

## Why It Adds Up

Relying on one channel means you’re only ever hearing from the customers who happen to be in front of a tablet at the right moment. Combine on-site with QR and email, and you start hearing from everyone else too — higher response rates, more complete data, and a much clearer picture of what’s actually going on across every touchpoint.

## Tl;dr:

Every FeedbackPro device license now includes two active surveys — one for in-the-moment, on-device feedback, and one you can share by email or QR code for feedback after the fact. Same license, twice the reach.
